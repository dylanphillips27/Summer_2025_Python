# Summer_2025_Python Overview

**1. ATM-Project.py**

  This is a fun, fake ATM machine. When you start, your account gets some random amount of fake money. You can depsoit more fake money into your account, or withdraw some fake money from your account. You also have to enter a PIN at the beggining to acess your account, and the PIN is 1234. There are also some other features, like the ability for the ATM to deposit a fake check, or the feature that putting the PIN in backwards locks you out and pretends to call the police. Overall, this is just a fun little project with lots of different aspects, and is pretty fun to play with.

**2. MessageEncoderProject.py**

  This is a project that allows you to enter a message and then encrypt it, and you can also decrypt a message generated by this same machine. The encoding process translates any charecter, number, or punctuation into a given number and then adds a random number to everything. The number is printed at the beggining of the code, and you input that as the key, and the machine can then translate the encoded message back into letters, numbers, and symbols to show the original message.

**3. RockPaperScissorsGame.py**

  This is rock paper scissors where you play against the computer, and the computer uses a randomly generated play (rock, paper, or sicssors). It can also track wins/losses for the user and computer. 

**4. SimpleCalculator.py**

  This calculator can do addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division. A unique feature is that you can actually type in the action you want it to preform (ex. "I want to add something"), and then put in the numbers (ex. 2), and it will translate the text into one of the four actions it could take. It also keeps track of your total calculations and can print out all your calculations you have preformed when you are done with the calculator. 

**5. TemperatureConverter.py**

  This temperature converter converts temperature in either Kelvin, Farenheit, or Celsius into another form of temperature. You have to chose which conversion you like and input the starting number. It is also designed so that you cannot input a number that is not possible (ex. inputting -1 for the temperature in Kelvin).

**6. TicTacToeGame.py**

  This is a game where you play tic tac toe against another player on a matrix made of nested lists. X is player 1 and O is player 2. You take turns playing the game, and can win in any of the eight possible win conditions, or you can tie. The computer can also keep track of wins for each player and total ties for as many rounds as you play for.
